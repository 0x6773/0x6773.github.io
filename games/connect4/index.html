Connect Four

Drop discs, connect 4 to win!

How to Play
Players take turns dropping colored discs into the grid
Discs fall to the lowest available row
Connect 4 discs in a row — horizontally, vertically, or diagonally — to win
Keys: 1–7 or ← → then Enter; U undo, M music
The first move alternates each game; a full board is a draw
