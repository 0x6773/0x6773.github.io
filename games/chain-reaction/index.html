Your turn

CHAIN REACTION

Fill a cell to bursting and set off a chain reaction

How to Play
Take turns adding an orb to an empty cell or one of yours
A cell bursts when its orbs match its neighbours: corners 2, edges 3, others 4
Bursts capture the cells around them and can set off chains
Lose every orb and you're out; the last player standing wins
Keys: arrows + Enter to place, U to undo, M for music
