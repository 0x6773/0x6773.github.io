Minesweeper

Classic mine-clearing puzzle

How to Play
Click or tap to reveal; right click or long-press to flag
The flag button (or F) makes taps place flags
Numbers show adjacent mine count; click a number to chord
Clear all non-mine cells to win; first click is always safe
Hint (H) reveals a safe cell; a guess costs 10 seconds
4 difficulties: Easy, Medium, Hard, Custom · M toggles music
